It already works.

Plug it into any USB port, charger or battery. No app, no account, no setup.

It sits on your desk and blinks. When you run the optional program below, it starts showing what your Codex session is doing, and gets your attention when Codex is waiting on a decision from you.

Put your name on it

ls /dev/cu.usbmodem*
printf '{"name":"Your Name"}\n' > /dev/cu.usbmodemXXXX

Use whichever path the first command printed. It remembers.

Optional: your live Codex session

npx github:sammarcus/codex-companion

One file, no dependencies, nothing installed. It reads counters and timestamps, never your code or your prompts, and it can never approve anything on your behalf. Ctrl-C ends it completely.
